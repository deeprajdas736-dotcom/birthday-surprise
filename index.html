Gift No. 02

Open it ✨
01

Those Eyes

Before you knew you were being noticed.

Long before all these memories had names, there was something about you that was impossible not to notice.

Your eyes.

Ridiculously beautiful, yes. But somehow they carry curiosity, confidence, mischief and a hundred thoughts all at once.

There was a time when I would quietly steal a glance whenever you weren't looking.

And the funny thing is, as beautiful as those eyes are, the person behind them turned out to be even more interesting.

02

Important Evidence

Of course...

This is also the same highly sophisticated, extremely serious, deeply intellectual person we are talking about.

Exhibit A. 😌

And I wouldn't remove this version of you from the story for anything.

03

That Smile

Some smiles make a picture better.

Yours somehow makes things feel calmer.

There was once a boy who could barely ask you a simple question without fumbling half his words.

Somewhere along the way, that same smile became associated with courage.

Poster competitions. Science-model competitions. New ideas. Moments of doubt.

You have this strange ability to make people believe they should at least try.

04

A Familiar Face

Somehow conversations became their own place.

Delhi gave us calls, video calls, wandering from shop to shop, looking for something good to eat...

shawarma, kebab rolls, momos, mango shakes...

Small things. Completely ordinary things.

But a normal day has a different colour when the right person is in it.

05

The Lala Buddhi

Beauty was never your only dangerous feature.

Then there is the famous Shrivastava Lala Buddhi.

Sharp. Curious. Difficult to fool. Very capable of forming your own opinion.

You are one of those people around whom others think twice before saying something completely ulta-pulta.

Never make yourself smaller just to make somebody else comfortable.

06

Free Bird

There has never been a cage made for you.

Of all the things that make you who you are, this may be one of the biggest.

You are your own person.

You need room to think, choose, explore, change your mind, make mistakes, laugh and follow your curiosity.

You were never meant to fit inside somebody else's definition of who you should be.

Keep flying.

07

Open Skies

Some people simply look right beneath an open sky.

Maybe because freedom suits you.

Maybe because places feel more alive when you're curious enough to actually experience them.

The best version of you has always been the one that gets to be free.

08

Ordinary Days

You have a habit of making normal days worth remembering.

College. Scooty rides. Roads. Conversations. Completely ordinary evenings.

None of these things should sound extraordinary.

Yet somehow places become memories because you were there.

09

The Courage You Give

Sometimes courage doesn't arrive as a speech.

Sometimes it is simply one person saying:

“Try.”

And somehow making you believe that maybe you actually can.

You have been that person more times than you probably realise.

I hope life gives that same courage back to you whenever you need it.

10

Entirely Your Own

Strong doesn't have to mean loud.

Sometimes it is simply knowing where you stand.

Knowing what you believe.

Knowing when to speak.

And knowing when somebody is talking complete nonsense.

Stay difficult to underestimate.

11

Just Geography

Life keeps changing the map.

Gwalior. Delhi. Kolkata. Agartala.

Distances grow, schedules change, and life continues moving.

But distance can change where someone is. It cannot change what their existence adds to your life.

And yours has added courage, laughter, ideas, memories and a lot more colour.

So today...

This isn't about our story.

It's about you.

The girl with unforgettable eyes.

That wonderfully soothing smile.

The famous Lala Buddhi.

The courage.

The independence.

The mind that refuses to accept everything it is told.

And that wonderfully untameable spirit.

Happy Birthday.

I hope this year gives you bigger spaces to grow, work that excites your mind, people who respect your freedom, adventures you haven't planned yet, and countless reasons for that smile to appear.

Keep learning.

Keep questioning.

Keep laughing.

Keep flying.
